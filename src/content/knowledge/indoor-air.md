Indoor particles come from outside air that leaks in and from sources such as cooking and unflued combustion. A steady-state mass balance is used: outdoor air enters, particles deposit or are removed by a purifier, and indoor sources add mass. A correctly sized purifier and one closed room reduce the space you have to clean.
