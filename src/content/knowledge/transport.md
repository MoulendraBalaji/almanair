title: Climate emissions and local smoke are different
tags: transport car bus walk bike emissions co2
sources: defra-ghg-2024
---
A car trip emits climate-warming CO2 and also produces local pollutants. Walking and cycling have no operational emissions. Official CO2e factors quantify the climate effect; local pollutant effects are described qualitatively here because a single universal gram-per-kilometre figure is not available.
