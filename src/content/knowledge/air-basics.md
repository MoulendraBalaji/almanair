An AQI band tells you how cautious to be, not exactly how you will feel. Use the band with your own plan: who is sensitive, how long you will be outside, and whether you have a filtered room. The WHO 24-hour PM2.5 guideline is 15 micrograms per cubic metre; it is a guideline, not a safe threshold.
