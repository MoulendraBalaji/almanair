id: purifier-sizing
title: Sizing a purifier for a room
tags: purifier cadr ach room volume clean air
sources: epa-air-cleaners
---
Match the clean-air delivery rate to the room. In SI units clean air changes per hour equal the purifier's cubic metres per hour divided by the room's cubic metres. A higher rate cleans faster; a filter only works if the room is closed and the fan actually runs.
