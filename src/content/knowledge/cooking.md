title: Cooking, heating and carbon monoxide
tags: cooking stove kerosene biomass carbon monoxide safety
sources: who-household-air, cdc-carbon-monoxide
---
Cooking and unflued heating can dominate indoor particle exposure. Use a lid, extract fumes outside where safe, and never burn charcoal, kerosene or biomass in living space without a flue. Carbon monoxide has no smell; a detector and fresh air are essential.
